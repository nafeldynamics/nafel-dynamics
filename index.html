What We Do

Eight Core
Service Pillars

We don't just run trainings. We design transformation programs with clear systems, measurable KPIs, and documented outcomes.

01

Enterprise Development

We help businesses grow properly

We work with small and medium businesses that are stuck — fixing poor records, weak pricing, absent systems, and operational chaos. We restructure, formalise, and build the foundations for sustainable growth.

02

Capacity Building

We train people to perform better

Targeted training programs for business owners, staff, cooperatives, and founders. From bookkeeping to governance — we build skills that stick and change behaviour, not just fill attendance sheets.

03

MSME Support

We give small businesses real tools to survive and scale

Access to finance readiness, business diagnostics, compliance support, and market linkages. We prepare small businesses to qualify for bank loans, meet regulatory demands, and connect to buyers.

04

Ecosystem Strengthening

We improve the environment around businesses, not just the businesses

We work with governments, banks, and incubators to design SME policy frameworks, build startup incubation models, and develop cluster strategies. Higher-level, strategic work. Bigger, longer contracts.

05

Financial Literacy

We teach people how to manage money wisely

Financial education programs for youth, women entrepreneurs, farmers, and low-income communities. Digital savings training, personal finance bootcamps, and cashflow management — designed for real comprehension.

06

Value Chain Promotion

We help industries work better from start to finish

We map, analyse, and strengthen entire value chains — coordinating players from farmer to retailer, improving quality, reducing waste, and opening market access. Built on field research and stakeholder engagement.

07

Management Consulting

We solve serious business problems for companies

Our premium service. Strategy development, organisational restructuring, performance improvement, financial restructuring, and risk assessment. This is where our margins are strongest and our impact is deepest.

08

HRM / Training Services

Full lifecycle HR — from hire to retire, and everything in between

We deliver end-to-end human resource solutions: recruitment, background checks, onboarding and offboarding, payroll management, training, and retraining programs. We also design and set up full HR departments from scratch for growing organisations. For businesses not yet ready for a full-time HR lead, our Fractional HRM and Fractional CFO services provide executive-level people and finance leadership on a flexible, cost-effective basis.

Our Signature Methodology

The Nafel Dynamics
S.T.E.A.D.Y Framework™

Our proprietary framework applied to enterprises, institutions, value chains, and government agencies. Structure, not vibes.

STEADY
S

Systems Diagnosis

Business audit, governance assessment, financial structure review, and market positioning analysis. We establish what's actually wrong before recommending anything.

→ Output: Structural gap report
T

Targeted Strategy Design

Growth model selection, financial structuring plan, operational improvement roadmap, and policy alignment for institutions. We design before we execute.

→ Output: Strategic action blueprint
E

Execution Strengthening

SOP development, capacity building, process optimisation, and financial system installation. We build systems that run without us present.

→ Output: Functional systems in place
A

Access Enablement

Market linkages, capital readiness, institutional partnerships, and ecosystem integration. We open doors that businesses cannot open alone.

→ Output: Growth acceleration channels activated
D

Data & Performance Tracking

KPI dashboards, financial tracking, monitoring tools, and impact measurement. We are not done until results are documented and visible.

→ Output: Measurable performance visibility
Y

Yield & Sustainability

Institutionalisation of processes, governance strengthening, scalability design, and transition planning. Growth must outlast our engagement.

→ Output: Long-term sustainability
